\section{Requirements and quality goals}
\label{chap:requirements-quality-goals}

The quality goals are portability, product protection, idempotence, rollback,
platform independence, reproducible export, versioned migration, and verifiable
documentation. A \CheckMode{} must remain read-only; a \FullFix{} may execute only an
operation that was visible in a prior plan.

Product protection & Unknown and product-owned files stay unchanged. \\

\bottomrule

These goals are requirements, not a blanket claim about every future adapter
implementation. Their structure check remains \Planned{} until it can be tied to the
