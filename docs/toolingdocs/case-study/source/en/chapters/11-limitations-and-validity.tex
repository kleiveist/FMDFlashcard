\section{Limitations and validity}
\label{chap:limitations-validity}

The case study does not cover every technology, filesystem, or real customer environment.
Package registries, operating-system variation, simulated fixtures, and the semantic
correctness of an allowlisted adapter are outside the transaction guarantee. Process
containment is not a complete sandbox either.

Local PDF results are currently a \Limitation{} because the audited TeX environment is
not installed in this work container. The sources and CI steps are designed to produce
that evidence reproducibly later; missing metrics are not estimated.
