\section{Introduction}
\label{chap:introduction}

A portable tooling directory is not a product template. It must not claim ownership of
an existing application, domain data, or unknown files. This case study therefore asks
how copyable tooling can support several project shapes without owning product sources or
hiding its changes. Its method follows a traceable case-study perspective
\parencite{runeson2009case-study}.

The scope covers context resolution, profiles, adapters, transactions, state, migration,
export, and tests in the current tooling revision \parencite{template-tooling-source}.
The remaining chapters distinguish observed implementation, automated verification,
future work, and limitations explicitly.
