\section{Conclusion and outlook}
\label{chap:conclusion-outlook}

The study answers its guiding question through explicit boundaries: context rather than a
fixed root, capabilities rather than a product scaffold, declared adapters rather than
guesswork, and transactions rather than uncontrolled filesystem changes. Those properties
support a replaceable tooling payload without appropriating product sources.

Next steps are additional adapter contracts, real platform-specific measurement runs, an
updated evidence collection, and a renewed template audit whenever the technical contract
changes. These activities are \Planned{}, not claims about an already achieved state.
